# Open questions for the site owner

The content is written without these claims until they're answered. Write an answer under
a question and the next daily run will use it.

1. **Founding year:** the About page says both "since 1995" and "Established in 2006". Which is correct?
2. **Transit times:** the town pages say "sea transit approximately 14–28 days" for every
   country, including the USA and Mexico. What are the real typical transit times per region
   (GCC, Europe, Americas, Africa, South-East Asia)?
3. **Ex-stock dispatch:** is "24–48 hours" still accurate, and for which items or sizes?
4. **MOQ:** is there a minimum order quantity or value for export orders?
5. **Export track record:** which countries have you actually shipped flanges to? Can past
   projects or industries be named (without client names)?
6. **Approvals:** do you hold any end-user vendor approvals (Aramco, ADNOC, KOC, QatarEnergy,
   PDO), PED or other third-party certifications? They won't be claimed unless you confirm.
7. **Duplicate country page:** `/export/usa/` and `/export/united-states/` target the same
   searches. Can `/export/usa/` be redirected (301) to `/export/united-states/`? Until you
   decide, both stay and are written differently.
8. **Incoterms:** which do you offer (EXW, FOB Nhava Sheva, CIF, DAP)?
9. **"Get Quote in 30 Min":** the navbar and several pages promise a quote within 30
   minutes, and the flange pages said "standard orders dispatched in 7–14 days". Are both
   accurate? I removed them from the pages I rewrote and left the navbar button as it is.
10. **Coatings and stub ends:** do you supply coated (galvanised or epoxy) flanges, and stub
    ends, or only bare forged flanges? The pages currently say "state it in the RFQ and the
    quotation confirms it".
11. **US exports and duties:** have you shipped flanges to the USA, and do you have a company-specific
    AD/CVD rate or entry history? The US pages tell buyers to check the rate with their broker.
12. **ThomasNet:** the old `/export/usa/` page said "ThomasNet Listed Manufacturer". Is there a real
    listing? It was removed until confirmed.
13. **Regional standards:** can you make flanges to KS B 1503 (Korea) and SANS 1123 (South Africa)
    tables, and supply EN grades such as P250GH and 1.4404 with PED documentation? The pages say
    "quoted against your specification" and claim neither.
14. **Letters of credit:** can export documents be prepared to match a buyer's LC (Bangladesh,
    Nigeria)? The pages say only that we supply the MTC and shipping documents.
15. **Classification societies:** can you arrange KR, ABS, DNV or Lloyd's Register witnessing? The

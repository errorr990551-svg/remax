# Open questions for the site owner

The content is written without these claims until they're answered. Write an answer under
a question and the next daily run will use it.

1. **Founding year:** the About page says both "since 1995" and "Established in 2006". Which is correct?
2. **Transit times:** the town pages say "sea transit approximately 14–28 days" for every
   country, including the USA and Mexico. What are the real typical transit times per region
   (GCC, Europe, Americas, Africa, South-East Asia)?
3. **Ex-stock dispatch:** is "24–48 hours" still accurate, and for which items or sizes?
4. **MOQ:** is there a minimum order quantity or value for export orders?
5. **Export track record:** which countries have you actually shipped flanges to? Can past
   projects or industries be named (without client names)?
6. **Approvals:** do you hold any end-user vendor approvals (Aramco, ADNOC, KOC, QatarEnergy,
   PDO), PED or other third-party certifications? They won't be claimed unless you confirm.
7. **Duplicate country page:** `/export/usa/` and `/export/united-states/` target the same
   searches. Can `/export/usa/` be redirected (301) to `/export/united-states/`? Until you
   decide, both stay and are written differently.
8. **Incoterms:** which do you offer (EXW, FOB Nhava Sheva, CIF, DAP)?
